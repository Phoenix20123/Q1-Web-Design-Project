About Us

About us- The story of Ironhoopers, a highly resilient shoe brand, began in the industrial neighborhoods of Newark, New Jersey, where the company originally came to life as a specialized workshop dedicated to crafting indestructible footwear. Founded in the mid-20th century, the brand was born out of a desire to fuse the raw strength of heavy-duty industrial work boots with the flexible agility required for competitive athletics—effectively creating footwear and slidesbuilt like iron for players "hooping" on rugged outdoor urban courts. The company expanded its footprint across the United States by securing pivotal distribution contracts with regional manufacturing plants and grassroots basketball leagues, gradually transitioning from a small-scale Northeast operation into a prominent national name. As the brand scaled and modernized its corporate operations, it strategically relocated, and its headquarters is now in Atlanta, Georgia. Today, Ironhoopers occupies a unique and highly respected niche in the global shoe industry, widely celebrated for blending its rugged, retro Newark roots with high-performance athletic engineering that appeals equally to modern streetwear collectors and traditional trade workers.
